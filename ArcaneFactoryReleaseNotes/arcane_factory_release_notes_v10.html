Core Gameplay & Automation 3 updates

CGA-151

Harvested resources drop inside the tree model

Weapon-hit drops now land beside the tree’s drawn build_bounds, clear of neighbouring trees. Repeated hits still stack in one pile.

CGA-155

Wants planks to work as fuel

Planks now fuel furnaces, burning for 2 seconds versus 4 seconds for logs.

CGA-166

The buggy hard-stops on many objects

The Buggy now knocks trees over (1 damage each, no logs) and pushes small rocks and debris aside (no yield). The Plough still fells trees and harvests logs.

Combat & World Unrest 2 updates

CWU-76

Combat music fades in and out too slowly

Combat music enters in 0.5 seconds (previously 0.8) and stops 3 seconds after quiet (previously 9).

CWU-80

Jumping while shooting leaves a duplicate pistol projectile

Removed the duplicate pistol bolt effect. Jumping no longer separates the projectile visuals.

Player Character & Animation 1 updates

PCA-41

Teleport mana is spent on cast start, not on teleport

Mana is checked when casting begins, but only spent when teleport completes. Cancelled or interrupted casts cost nothing.

Research & Progression 3 updates

RP-45

The furnace and iron ingot objective does not complete

The objective still requires placing the Mechanical Assembler. Once materials are ready, it now says “Materials ready: place the Mechanical Assembler now…” rather than appearing stuck.

RP-47

Research doesn’t say when a project needs the Advanced Analysis Station

The research card hover and tracker now identify the required station when you do not own one of that tier.

RP-49

Research requirements already met still look unmet

Completed prerequisites now display green “Has X” tags instead of amber “Needs X”.

UI / UX & Foundry 16 updates

UUF-146

The fabricator’s message covers the character when zoomed in

The speech bubble anchors at head height instead of ground level, clearing the character model at any zoom.

UUF-150

Mobs draw over other map icons

Creature markers now render beneath clues, POIs, resources, machines and pins on the map and minimap.

UUF-155

Wants shift-click to move materials out of machine slots

Already supported: shift-click moves the entire stack out. Behaviour verified and covered by a test; no code change required.

UUF-160

Tab does not close the inventory

Tab now closes the side-by-side satchel/fabricator at widths of 1280 px and above; on narrow windows it still switches tabs.

UUF-167

Menus should draw above other interface elements

Satchel, trunk and pause panels now sit above the HUD, action bar and prompts using MENU_Z.

UUF-170

Abandoned factory storage has no take all button

Added Take all to abandoned-factory storage. Overflow remains in the piece and dismantling stays locked until it is empty.

UUF-172

“Commit repair materials” says there is no iron while holding iron ingots

No inventory recognition bug reproduced. The refusal now names the exact required item and quantity and points to nearby storage.

UUF-174

POI wording implies emptying dismantles it

Both storage messages now explicitly state that emptying containers does not dismantle the find or piece.

UUF-175

POI icon stays on the map after dismantling

Dismantled sites no longer show markers or labels on the world map or minimap, including after saving and reloading.

UUF-181

Water renders above projectiles

Native effects now draw after water, so projectiles remain visible above the surface.

UUF-187

Says you do not have the arcane core while holding it

As with UUF-172, the relic refusal now specifies “Needs 1 × Arcane Core.” and points to nearby storage.

UUF-193

Shift-click to move items in and out of the buggy inventory

Added missing click handlers. Shift-click moves a whole stack between trunk and satchel in either direction; Ctrl moves half.

UUF-195

The buggy inventory is hard to find

While driving or standing beside the Buggy, the vehicle prompt now says “Tab: satchel + trunk”.

UUF-196

The red hurt glow appears when getting out of the buggy

Health now tracks its source, preventing false damage flashes when switching from vehicle to driver health.

UUF-198

Quick stack inventory into nearby warehouses

New Quick stack button beside Sort backpack. It transfers backpack items to nearby warehouses already holding that item, leaves the hotbar alone and explains what could not move.

UUF-199

Worlds screen should show when a save was made

The Worlds screen now shows “Last saved” using the save’s timestamp, rather than the last-played time.
